# Pear and Almond Frangipane Tart

## Description
This baked tart combines crisp shortcrust pastry, a soft almond filling, and tender pears. Frangipane is a sweet almond-based filling often used in pastries. Each stage can be prepared ahead. Serve the tart at room temperature after the filling has set.

## Recipe summary
- Preparation: 45 minutes, plus 30 minutes chilling
- Baking: 45–55 minutes
- Cooling: At least 45 minutes
- Serves: 8
- Difficulty: Moderate
- Equipment: 23 cm tart tin with removable base, rolling pin, baking paper, baking weights or dried beans

## Ingredients

### Shortcrust pastry
- 200 g plain flour
- 100 g cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 40 g icing sugar
- 1 small egg yolk
- 1–2 tbsp cold water
- Pinch of salt

### Almond filling
- 120 g softened unsalted butter
- 120 g caster sugar
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 150 g ground almonds
- 20 g plain flour
- 1/2 tsp almond extract, optional
- Finely grated zest of 1/2 lemon

### Pear topping
- 3 ripe but firm pears
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp apricot jam
- 1 tsp hot water

Optional for serving: icing sugar, crème fraîche, or lightly whipped cream.

## Method

### Stage 1: Make the pastry
1. Put flour, icing sugar, and salt in a mixing bowl.
2. Add cold butter and rub it into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture resembles fine breadcrumbs.
3. Add the egg yolk and one tablespoon of cold water. Mix gently until the dough begins to come together. Add the second tablespoon only if needed.
4. Press the dough into a flat disc. Avoid kneading extensively, which can make pastry tough.
5. Wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

### Stage 2: Line and blind-bake the shell
1. Heat the oven to 180°C conventional or 160°C fan.
2. Roll the pastry on a lightly floured surface into a circle about 3 mm thick.
3. Lift it into the tart tin and press gently into the base and sides without stretching. Trim the edges.
4. Prick the base lightly with a fork. If the pastry has become warm, chill the lined tin for 10 minutes.
5. Line with baking paper and fill with baking weights or dried beans.
6. Bake for 15 minutes. Remove the paper and weights carefully, then bake for another 5–8 minutes until the base looks dry and begins to color.
7. Remove from the oven and let it cool slightly. Keep the oven on.

### Stage 3: Prepare the almond filling
1. Beat the softened butter and caster sugar until pale and creamy, about 2–3 minutes.
2. Add eggs one at a time, beating after each addition. If the mixture looks slightly curdled, continue; the ground almonds will help bring it together.
3. Fold in ground almonds, flour, almond extract if using, and lemon zest.
4. Mix just until evenly combined.

### Stage 4: Prepare the pears
1. Peel and core the pears, then cut them in half.
2. Slice each half lengthwise into thin slices. Keep the slices together if you want to fan them decoratively.
3. Toss lightly with lemon juice to reduce browning.
4. If the pears are very juicy, blot them with kitchen paper to avoid adding excess moisture.

### Stage 5: Assemble and bake
1. Spread the almond filling evenly in the partially baked pastry shell.
2. Arrange the pears over the filling, pressing them gently into the surface without fully submerging them.
3. Place the tart on a baking tray and bake for 30–40 minutes, until the filling is puffed and golden. The center should feel set rather than liquid.
4. If the pastry edges brown too quickly, cover them loosely with foil.
5. Cool in the tin for 15 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

### Stage 6: Glaze and serve
1. Warm the apricot jam with one teaspoon of hot water until smooth.
2. Brush a thin layer over the pears and exposed filling.
3. Allow the tart to cool to room temperature before slicing.
4. Dust with icing sugar if desired and serve with crème fraîche or lightly whipped cream.

## Troubleshooting
- **Pastry shrinks:** It may have been stretched or not chilled sufficiently. Rest the dough and press it into the tin without pulling.
- **Soggy base:** Blind-bake the shell properly and blot juicy pears.
- **Filling browns before it sets:** Lower the oven temperature by 10°C and cover loosely with foil.
- **Filling looks curdled:** This can happen when butter and eggs are at different temperatures. Continue with the recipe; the dry ingredients usually help.

## Make-ahead and storage
The pastry dough can be made up to 2 days ahead and refrigerated, or frozen for up to 2 months. Store the baked tart covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bring it to room temperature before serving.

## Variations
- Replace pears with peeled apple slices or halved plums.
- Add 40 g dark chocolate chips to the almond filling.
- Replace lemon zest with orange zest.
- Scatter 25 g flaked almonds over the tart before baking.

## Food safety note
Refrigerate the tart if it will not be eaten within a couple of hours, especially in a warm room. Allow it to cool before covering to reduce condensation.
