# Fluffy American-Style Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 200 g plain flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 250 ml milk
- 1 large egg
- 30 g melted butter
- Butter or neutral oil for frying

For serving:
- Maple syrup
- Fresh berries
- Powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. Combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients.
4. Stir just until no large pockets of dry flour remain. A few small lumps are fine. Do not overmix.
5. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and lightly grease it.
6. Pour approximately 60 ml of batter into the pan for each pancake.
7. Cook until bubbles appear across the surface and the edges look set, approximately 2 minutes.
8. Flip and cook the other side for 1–2 minutes.
9. Repeat with the remaining batter.

### Useful note

The first pancake is often less evenly browned because the pan is still reaching the correct temperature. Adjust the heat if later pancakes brown too quickly.

**Makes:** approximately 8 pancakes  
**Time:** 20 minutes
