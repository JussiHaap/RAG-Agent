# Finnish Salmon Soup (Lohikeitto)

A traditional Finnish-style creamy fish soup. The broth should remain light and delicate rather than becoming heavily seasoned.

## Ingredients

- 400 g salmon fillet, skin removed and cut into large cubes
- 500 g potatoes, peeled and cut into 2–3 cm cubes
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 1 leek, white and light-green parts, sliced
- 1 litre fish stock or light vegetable stock
- 200 ml cooking cream
- 2 bay leaves
- 6 whole allspice berries
- 1 small bunch fresh dill
- 20 g butter
- Salt
- White pepper or black pepper

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the leek and carrots. Cook gently for about 5 minutes. Avoid browning the vegetables.
3. Add the stock, potatoes, bay leaves, and allspice.
4. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat. Simmer for approximately 12–15 minutes, until the potatoes are almost tender.
5. Add the salmon pieces. Keep the soup at a gentle simmer rather than a rolling boil.
6. Cook for approximately 5 minutes, or until the salmon is just cooked through.
7. Stir in the cream and heat gently for 2–3 minutes.
8. Remove the bay leaves and allspice berries.
9. Taste before adding salt; the stock may already be salty.
10. Finish with a generous amount of chopped fresh dill.

## Serving

Serve hot with rye bread and butter.

## Notes

Cutting the salmon into fairly large pieces helps prevent it from becoming dry. If you prefer a thicker soup, mash a few of the cooked potato pieces against the side of the pot before adding the cream.

**Serves:** 4  
**Time:** 40 minutes
