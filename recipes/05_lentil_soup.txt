RED LENTIL SOUP

This is a simple, inexpensive soup that freezes well.

Serves 4
Time: 40 minutes

Ingredients
200 g red lentils
1 onion
2 carrots
2 cloves garlic
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp smoked or sweet paprika
800 ml vegetable stock
400 g canned chopped tomatoes
1 tbsp olive oil
Salt
Black pepper
Juice of half a lemon

Instructions
1. Rinse the lentils under cold water.
2. Heat olive oil in a large pot. Add onion and carrots and cook for 5–6 minutes.
3. Add garlic, cumin, and paprika. Stir for 30 seconds.
4. Add lentils, stock, and tomatoes.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 20–25 minutes.
6. When the lentils and carrots are soft, blend part of the soup with a stick blender. It is fine to leave some texture.
7. Season with salt and pepper. Add lemon juice just before serving.

Serving suggestion
Serve with toasted bread and a spoonful of Greek yogurt.

Storage
The soup keeps for about 3 days in the refrigerator and can be frozen in portions.
