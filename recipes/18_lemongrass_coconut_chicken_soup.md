# Lemongrass and Coconut Chicken Soup

## Description
This fragrant soup combines tender chicken, coconut milk, lemongrass, ginger, lime, and vegetables. It uses Southeast Asian-inspired flavors rather than claiming to be a strictly traditional regional recipe. The broth should be creamy, aromatic, savory, and bright with lime.

## Recipe details
- Preparation: 25 minutes
- Cooking: 30 minutes
- Total: About 55 minutes
- Serves: 4
- Difficulty: Moderate

## Ingredients

Aromatics:
- 2 stalks lemongrass
- 25 g fresh ginger, peeled and thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 small red chili, sliced, optional
- 2 shallots, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil

Soup base:
- 800 ml chicken stock
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 1 tbsp fish sauce, plus more to taste
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 2 kaffir lime leaves, optional
- 1 tbsp soy sauce, optional

Main ingredients:
- 400 g boneless chicken thighs or breast, thinly sliced
- 200 g mushrooms, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 150 g baby spinach or pak choi
- 150 g rice noodles

To finish:
- Juice of 1–2 limes
- Fresh coriander
- Sliced spring onions

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the lemongrass.** Remove dry outer leaves and trim the root ends. Bruise the lower, pale part of each stalk with the flat side of a knife to release its aroma. Cut into large pieces so they can be removed before serving.
2. **Cook the aromatics.** Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add shallots and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened. Add ginger, garlic, chili if using, and lemongrass. Stir for about one minute without allowing the garlic to brown.
3. **Infuse the stock.** Pour in the chicken stock. Add lime leaves, fish sauce, and brown sugar. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 10 minutes.
4. **Add coconut milk.** Stir in the coconut milk and keep the soup below a vigorous boil, as hard boiling can cause some coconut milk products to separate. Add soy sauce if you want a deeper savory flavor.
5. **Cook the chicken.** Add the chicken and simmer gently for 5–7 minutes, depending on thickness. Add mushrooms and bell pepper and cook for another 4–5 minutes. The chicken must be cooked through.
6. **Add greens.** Stir in spinach or pak choi for the final 1–2 minutes, until just wilted.
7. **Prepare noodles.** Cook rice noodles according to the packet directions. Some varieties need soaking, while others require boiling. Drain and divide among four bowls.
8. **Balance the broth.** Remove lemongrass and lime leaves. Stir in lime juice gradually, tasting as you go. Add fish sauce for saltiness or a little sugar if the flavor tastes too sharp.
9. **Serve.** Ladle soup over the noodles and garnish with coriander and spring onions.

## Substitutions
- No lemongrass: Use extra lime zest and ginger; the flavor will differ but remain fresh.
- No fish sauce: Use soy sauce, recognizing that the taste will change.
- Vegetarian version: Use vegetable stock, omit chicken and fish sauce, and add firm tofu. Season with soy sauce.
- Extra vegetables: Add thinly sliced carrots, snow peas, or baby corn.

## Make-ahead and storage
The broth can be made one day ahead without the noodles. Refrigerate promptly in a covered container for up to 3 days. Reheat until steaming hot and prepare noodles separately just before serving. Keeping noodles separate prevents them from absorbing too much broth and becoming soft.

## Cooking notes
Thinly slicing chicken helps it cook quickly without drying out. If using chicken breast, avoid simmering it longer than necessary. Add lime juice near the end to preserve its fresh aroma.
