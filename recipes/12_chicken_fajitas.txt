CHICKEN FAJITAS

Serves 4

INGREDIENTS

For the chicken:
- 400 g chicken breast, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and black pepper

Vegetables:
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil

To serve:
- 8 flour tortillas
- Lime wedges
- Sour cream
- Salsa
- Grated cheese

METHOD

1. Mix the chicken with oil, chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Leave for 10 minutes if you have time.
2. Heat a large frying pan until very hot.
3. Cook the chicken in a single layer. Depending on the size of the pan, work in batches. Cook for about 5–7 minutes until browned and cooked through.
4. Remove the chicken and set aside.
5. Add the second tablespoon of oil and cook the onion and peppers over high heat for 4–5 minutes. They should soften while retaining some texture.
6. Return the chicken to the pan and toss everything together.
7. Warm the tortillas briefly in a dry pan.
8. Serve with lime, sour cream, salsa, and cheese.

For extra smoky flavor, cook the vegetables until some edges become dark brown.
