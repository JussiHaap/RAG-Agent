Vegetable Stir-Fry

Serves 2 | 20 minutes

Ingredients:
- 1 carrot, thinly sliced
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 150 g broccoli
- 100 g sugar snap peas
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
- Cooked rice or noodles

Directions:
Heat the cooking oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat. Add the carrot and broccoli and cook for 2 minutes. Add the pepper and sugar snap peas.

Add the garlic and ginger and stir constantly for about 30 seconds. Pour in the soy sauce and sesame oil and toss everything together.

Serve immediately with rice or noodles.

Variation: Add tofu, cashews, mushrooms, or thinly sliced chicken.
