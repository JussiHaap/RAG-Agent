SIMPLE CHICKEN CURRY

Serves: 4
Preparation and cooking time: about 35 minutes

INGREDIENTS
400 g chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
1 large onion, chopped
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
1 tablespoon vegetable oil
2 tablespoons curry powder
1 teaspoon ground cumin
1 teaspoon turmeric
1 tablespoon tomato paste
400 ml coconut milk
150 ml chicken stock
1 red bell pepper, sliced
Salt and black pepper
Fresh coriander, optional
Cooked rice, to serve

METHOD
1. Heat the oil in a large, deep frying pan. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
2. Add garlic and ginger. Cook for another minute.
3. Stir in curry powder, cumin, turmeric, and tomato paste. Cook for about 30 seconds.
4. Add the chicken and stir until all pieces are coated with the spices.
5. Pour in the coconut milk and stock. Add the bell pepper.
6. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 15–18 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
7. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Serve over rice with coriander.

TIP
For a hotter curry, add a chopped chili with the garlic and ginger. For a milder version, use a mild curry powder and replace some of the coconut milk with extra stock.
