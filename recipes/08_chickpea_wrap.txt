Chickpea and Avocado Wrap

Serves 2
Time: 15 minutes

Ingredients:
1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
1 ripe avocado
2 large tortillas
1 tomato, diced
1/4 cucumber, diced
A handful of lettuce
2 tbsp Greek yogurt
1 tsp lemon juice
1/2 tsp paprika
Salt and black pepper

Method:
Mash the chickpeas roughly with a fork. Stir in the yogurt, lemon juice, paprika, salt, and pepper.

Spread the lettuce over the tortillas. Add the chickpea mixture, tomato, cucumber, and sliced avocado.

Fold the sides inward and roll the tortilla tightly. Cut each wrap in half.

Optional additions:
- Feta cheese
- Fresh mint
- Pickled red onion
- Hot sauce
