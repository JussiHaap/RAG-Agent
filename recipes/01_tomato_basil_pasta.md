# Tomato Basil Pasta

A quick weeknight pasta with a simple tomato sauce.

## Ingredients
- 250 g spaghetti
- 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Fresh basil
- 30 g Parmesan
- Salt and black pepper

## Instructions
1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water.
2. Fry the garlic in olive oil for 30 seconds.
3. Add tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Toss the drained pasta with the sauce.
6. Finish with basil and Parmesan.

**Time:** 25 minutes  
**Serves:** 2
