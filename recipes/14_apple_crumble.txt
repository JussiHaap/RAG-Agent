APPLE CRUMBLE

Serves 6
Preparation: 15 minutes
Baking: 35 minutes

INGREDIENTS

Filling:
5 medium apples
2 tablespoons granulated sugar
1 teaspoon cinnamon
1 tablespoon lemon juice

Crumble:
100 g plain flour
75 g rolled oats
80 g cold butter
60 g brown sugar
A pinch of salt

METHOD

1. Heat the oven to 190°C.
2. Peel and core the apples. Cut them into roughly equal slices.
3. Put the apples into a baking dish and toss with sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice.
4. To make the topping, mix flour, oats, brown sugar, and salt.
5. Cut the cold butter into small cubes. Rub it into the dry mixture with your fingertips until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs. A few larger pieces are desirable because they become crispy.
6. Scatter the topping over the apples.
7. Bake for 30–35 minutes. The topping should be golden brown and the fruit should be bubbling around the edges.
8. Rest for 5–10 minutes before serving.

Serve warm with vanilla ice cream, custard, or lightly whipped cream.

VARIATIONS

Replace half the apples with pears. For a nutty version, add 50 g chopped walnuts or almonds to the crumble topping.
