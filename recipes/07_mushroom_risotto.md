# Mushroom Risotto

Creamy risotto requires attention, but the technique is straightforward. Keep the stock hot throughout the cooking process and add it gradually rather than all at once.

## Ingredients

- 250 g Arborio or other risotto rice
- 300 g mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 750 ml vegetable or chicken stock
- 100 ml dry white wine
- 30 g butter
- 50 g Parmesan, finely grated
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Preparation

1. Put the stock in a saucepan and keep it just below a simmer.
2. Heat the olive oil in a wide, heavy pan. Add the mushrooms and cook over medium-high heat until they have released their moisture and started to brown. Season lightly and transfer them to a plate.
3. Lower the heat and add the onion to the same pan. Cook for 4–5 minutes until translucent.
4. Add the garlic and rice. Stir continuously for about one minute so the rice becomes lightly toasted.
5. Pour in the white wine. Stir until almost all of the liquid has evaporated.
6. Add one ladleful of hot stock. Stir regularly until the liquid has mostly been absorbed.
7. Continue adding stock one ladle at a time. Allow each addition to absorb before adding more. This usually takes 18–22 minutes.
8. Taste the rice. It should be tender but still have a slight firmness in the center. Add a little more stock if necessary.
9. Stir in the mushrooms and thyme.
10. Remove the pan from the heat. Add butter and Parmesan and stir vigorously for about 30 seconds.
11. Cover and rest for 2 minutes.
12. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.

## Troubleshooting

If the risotto becomes dry before the rice is cooked, add more hot stock. If it is too loose, let it stand for a minute or two; the rice will continue absorbing liquid.

**Serves:** 3–4  
**Total time:** 45 minutes
