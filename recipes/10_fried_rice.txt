VEGETABLE FRIED RICE

Serves: 3
Time: 20 minutes

The best fried rice is made with cold, cooked rice. Freshly cooked rice tends to be too moist and can become sticky in the pan.

Ingredients
- 3 cups cold cooked rice
- 2 eggs
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- 100 g frozen peas
- 2 spring onions
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- Black pepper

Instructions
1. Heat a large frying pan or wok until very hot.
2. Add the vegetable oil. Crack in the eggs and scramble quickly. Push the egg to one side.
3. Add the carrot and peas. Stir-fry for 2 minutes.
4. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
5. Add the cold rice. Use your spatula to break up any clumps.
6. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes, allowing the rice to contact the hot pan.
7. Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Mix thoroughly.
8. Return the scrambled egg to the center of the pan.
9. Add sliced spring onions and black pepper.
10. Serve immediately.

Optional: Add leftover chicken, prawns, tofu, corn, or finely sliced cabbage.
