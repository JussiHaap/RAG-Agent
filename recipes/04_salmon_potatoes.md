# Oven-Baked Salmon with Potatoes

## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets
- 500 g baby potatoes, halved
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried dill
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 150 g green beans
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to **200°C**.
2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tablespoon olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper.
3. Roast the potatoes for 20 minutes.
4. Add the salmon and green beans to the tray.
5. Drizzle the remaining oil over the salmon and season with dill, salt, and pepper.
6. Place lemon slices around the salmon.
7. Bake for another 12–15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily.
8. Serve immediately with the roasted potatoes and lemon.

### Tip

If the salmon fillets are particularly thick, allow a few extra minutes of cooking time. Avoid overcooking them; the center should remain moist.

**Serves:** 2  
**Total time:** approximately 40 minutes
