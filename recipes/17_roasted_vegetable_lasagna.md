ROASTED VEGETABLE LASAGNA

ABOUT
This lasagna layers roasted vegetables, tomato sauce, pasta sheets, and creamy cheese sauce. Roasting the vegetables first removes excess moisture and concentrates their flavor, helping the finished lasagna hold together when sliced.

Preparation: 35 minutes
Cooking: 55–65 minutes
Resting: 15 minutes
Serves: 6
Difficulty: Moderate

INGREDIENTS

Roasted vegetables:
- 1 medium aubergine, cut into 2 cm cubes
- 2 bell peppers, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 courgette, sliced into half-moons
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper

Tomato sauce:
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 700 g passata
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp balsamic vinegar, optional
- Salt and black pepper

Cheese sauce:
- 50 g butter
- 50 g plain flour
- 700 ml milk
- Pinch of ground nutmeg
- 100 g grated mozzarella
- 40 g grated Parmesan
- Salt and pepper

To assemble:
- 9–12 lasagna sheets, depending on the dish and packet instructions
- 100 g grated mozzarella for the top

METHOD

1. **Roast the vegetables.** Heat the oven to 220°C conventional or 200°C fan. Toss the aubergine, peppers, courgette, and onion with olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Spread them across a large tray, using two trays if crowded. Roast for 25–30 minutes, turning once.
2. **Make the tomato sauce.** Heat the olive oil in a saucepan. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds without browning it. Stir in tomato paste, passata, and basil. Simmer for 10–15 minutes, then season with salt, pepper, and optional balsamic vinegar.
3. **Make the cheese sauce.** Melt butter in a separate saucepan over medium-low heat. Whisk in flour and cook for 1–2 minutes. Gradually add milk, whisking after each addition to prevent lumps. Simmer for 5–8 minutes, stirring often, until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. Add nutmeg, mozzarella, Parmesan, salt, and pepper.
4. **Prepare the pasta.** Check the package instructions. Pre-cook sheets if required, then lay them flat on a clean tea towel. No-boil sheets can be used directly if the sauces are loose enough.
5. **Assemble.** Reduce the oven to 200°C conventional or 180°C fan. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce in a baking dish. Add pasta, roasted vegetables, tomato sauce, and cheese sauce. Repeat the layers and finish with cheese sauce. Scatter mozzarella over the top.
6. **Bake.** Bake for 35–45 minutes, until golden and bubbling. If the top browns too quickly, cover loosely with foil.
7. **Rest.** Leave the lasagna to stand for 15 minutes before slicing. This helps the layers settle.

SERVING SUGGESTIONS
Serve with a green salad dressed with lemon juice and olive oil. Garlic bread also works well.

MAKE-AHEAD
Roast the vegetables and make the tomato sauce up to one day ahead. The assembled, unbaked lasagna can be covered and refrigerated for up to 24 hours. Add extra baking time if it goes into the oven cold and ensure the center is piping hot.

VARIATIONS
- Add 200 g sliced mushrooms and roast them with the other vegetables.
- Add 150 g ricotta in small spoonfuls between the layers.
- For a vegan version, use plant-based milk and cheese and prepare the white sauce with olive oil or vegan butter.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- Watery lasagna: Roast the vegetables until they lose some moisture and simmer the tomato sauce until thickened.
- Lumpy cheese sauce: Add milk gradually while whisking. Blend briefly if necessary.
- Hard pasta edges: Ensure pasta is covered with sauce and follow the packet directions.
