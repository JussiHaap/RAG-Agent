# Baked Potatoes with Spicy Beans

## Ingredients

- 4 medium baking potatoes
- 1 can kidney beans, drained
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 100 ml water
- 150 g grated cheddar
- 100 g sour cream
- 2 spring onions
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

### 1. Bake the potatoes

Heat the oven to 220°C. Scrub the potatoes and dry them thoroughly. Pierce each potato several times with a fork.

Place directly on the oven rack or on a baking tray. Bake for 50–65 minutes, depending on size. They are ready when a knife slides easily into the center.

### 2. Make the beans

While the potatoes cook, heat a small amount of oil in a saucepan. Add the onion and cook for 4 minutes.

Add garlic, tomato paste, paprika, and chili powder. Stir for 30 seconds.

Add kidney beans and water. Simmer for 8–10 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.

### 3. Assemble

Cut a cross into the top of each potato and squeeze the sides gently to open it. Fluff the inside with a fork.

Add the spicy beans, cheddar, sour cream, and sliced spring onions.

**Serves:** 4  
**Total time:** about 70 minutes

**Shortcut:** Microwave the potatoes for 6–8 minutes before transferring them to a hot oven. They will finish faster, although the skin will be less crisp.
