# Slow-Cooked Beef Ragù

A rich tomato and beef sauce that develops flavor through a long, gentle simmer. It is ideal for pappardelle or tagliatelle and can be made a day ahead.

## Recipe overview
- Preparation: 25 minutes
- Cooking: 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours
- Serves: 4–6
- Difficulty: Moderate

## Ingredients
- 700 g beef chuck or stewing beef, cut into 2–3 cm cubes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- 1 celery stalk, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 150 ml dry red wine
- 800 g canned chopped tomatoes
- 250 ml beef stock
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 small rosemary sprig, optional
- Salt and black pepper
- 400–500 g pappardelle or tagliatelle
- 30 g grated Parmesan

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the beef.** Pat the beef dry with kitchen paper and season lightly with salt and pepper. Dry meat browns better than wet meat.
2. **Brown in batches.** Heat the olive oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat. Brown the beef on several sides, working in batches so the pan is not crowded. Transfer the beef to a plate.
3. **Cook the vegetables.** Reduce the heat to medium. Add onion, carrot, and celery and cook for 7–9 minutes until soft. Stir occasionally and scrape up browned bits from the bottom. Add garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.
4. **Toast the tomato paste.** Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1–2 minutes. Pour in the red wine and scrape the bottom of the pot. Simmer for 2–3 minutes, until the wine has reduced by about half.
5. **Add the liquids.** Add tomatoes, stock, bay leaf, oregano, and rosemary if using. Return the beef and any juices to the pot.
6. **Simmer slowly.** Bring to a gentle simmer, then reduce the heat to low. Cover partially and cook for 2½–3 hours. Stir every 20–30 minutes. If the sauce becomes too thick before the meat is tender, add a little water or stock.
7. **Check the texture.** The beef should break apart easily with a spoon. Remove the bay leaf and rosemary stem. Shred large pieces with two forks and stir the meat back into the sauce.
8. **Cook the pasta.** Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve a mug of pasta water before draining.
9. **Finish the dish.** Toss the pasta with the ragù over low heat for 1–2 minutes. Add a splash of pasta water if needed. Serve with Parmesan.

## Troubleshooting
- **Beef is tough:** Continue simmering. Tough cuts need time for connective tissue to soften.
- **Sauce is watery:** Simmer uncovered until excess liquid evaporates.
- **Sauce is too thick:** Add water, stock, or pasta water a little at a time.
- **Sauce tastes acidic:** Simmer longer and taste again before adding a small pinch of sugar.

## Storage and variations
Refrigerate leftovers in a sealed container for up to 3 days, or freeze in portions for up to 3 months. For a deeper flavor, add 100 ml milk after the wine has reduced. Finely chopped mushrooms can be added with the vegetables.
