# Creamy Tomato Soup

This version uses canned tomatoes and is designed as an easy lunch or starter.

## Ingredients

- 800 g canned tomatoes
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 100 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a saucepan.
2. Add the onion and cook gently for about 5 minutes.
3. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add tomatoes, stock, basil, and sugar.
5. Simmer uncovered for 20 minutes.
6. Blend until smooth using a stick blender or countertop blender.
7. Return the soup to low heat and stir in the cream.
8. Season with salt and black pepper. Do not boil after adding the cream.

## Serving ideas

Serve with grilled cheese sandwiches, toasted sourdough, or croutons.

**Serves:** 4
