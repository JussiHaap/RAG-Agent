# Beef Tacos

A fast dinner that can be assembled at the table.

## Ingredients

### Beef
- 400 g ground beef
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- Salt and pepper

### To serve
- 8 small tortillas
- Shredded lettuce
- 1 tomato, diced
- 80 g grated cheese
- Sour cream
- Lime wedges
- Fresh coriander, optional

## Instructions

1. Cook the onion in a dry frying pan for 3–4 minutes.
2. Add the beef and break it apart with a wooden spoon. Cook until browned.
3. Stir in the spices and tomato paste. Cook for 2 minutes.
4. Taste and season with salt and pepper.
5. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
6. Let everyone assemble their own tacos with the beef and toppings.

**Serves:** 4  
**Time:** 25 minutes
